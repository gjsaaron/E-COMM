Product Description

Experience the pinnacle of diecast excellence with the Mini GT Nissan GT-R R35 Nismo. This meticulously crafted 1:64 scale replica captures every detail of the legendary Japanese supercar, from its aggressive aero package to its authentic Nismo racing livery.

Each model features a fully die-cast metal body with opening hood and doors, revealing an intricately detailed engine bay and interior. The rubber tires are mounted on precision-cast wheels that perfectly replicate the real car's design. Every decal and sponsor logo is faithfully reproduced using high-quality tampo printing.
